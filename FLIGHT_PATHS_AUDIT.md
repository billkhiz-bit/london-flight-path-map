## Methodology notes

- **Sampling:** 50 points evenly spaced along the polyline by great-circle distance.
- **CRS:** WGS84 → EPSG:27700 (British National Grid) via pyproj.
- **NoData / below-threshold pixels:** treated as 35 dB (DEFRA only publishes contours ≥40 dB Lden; below-threshold = quiet by construction).
- **Bbox:** DEFRA Round 4 covers England only. Paths stretching into Scotland / NI / out to sea will show low % in bbox.
- **Reproducibility:** re-run `python scripts/audit_flight_paths.py` after any change to FLIGHT_PATHS in `index.html` (also update the mirrored copy at the top of this script).
