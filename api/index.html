What you get

0-10 score per postcode

UK postcodes (33 London boroughs, expansion underway) and NYC ZIPs (~182 residential). Single composite + four components: quiet, affordability, growth, liveability.

Methodology fully published

Every threshold anchored to a published source: DEFRA Strategic Noise Mapping, DfE Progress 8, ONS, HM Land Registry HPI, EU Environmental Noise Directive 2002/49/EC. No AI summaries layered on top.

Per-postcode noise resolution

Quiet is scored per postcode from Haversine proximity to airports and flight-path geometry, at the postcode centroid rather than a borough average. The response reports which tier answered in context.quietResolution.

Note (August 2026): the DEFRA Lden raster tier described in our methodology is currently bypassed while a coverage problem is resolved — DEFRA's aircraft contours cover only about a tenth of London, and treating the unmapped remainder as quiet flattened the component. quietResolution therefore returns postcode, never raster, until that is fixed.
