Thank you for reviewing Sky Score 1.0.22. This update brings the app in line with our website.

1. WHAT THE APP DOES
Sky Score shows what official figures say about a place: aircraft and road noise, air quality, flood risk and the planes overhead, beside prices, crime, schools, transport and healthcare. It covers 97 council areas in 13 UK city regions, plus New York's 5 boroughs and 50 Bay Area cities. Every figure names its source and its year.

2. SECTION 4.2 (MINIMUM FUNCTIONALITY)
- Score where I am: a location button on the map, shown only in the app, finds the UK postcode you are standing in and opens its answer in one tap: for viewing a home or walking an area without knowing the postcode.
- A layout made for the app: tabs and the map as the background, used only in the native app.
- A native splash screen and status bar.

3. HOW TO TRY IT
No account or login is needed. Search a UK postcode, for example TW4 6EX (under the Heathrow approach), a council area such as Hounslow, or a Bay Area city such as Palo Alto. Score where I am finds UK postcodes; outside the UK it says so and suggests searching instead.

4. LOCATION
Location is read only when you tap Score where I am ("When In Use"). The coordinates go to api.postcodes.io, a free UK postcode lookup, to find the nearest postcode, and are not stored. If permission is denied, everything else works.

5. PRIVACY
The privacy policy is at https://skyscore.co.uk/privacy. No ads, no tracking, and no advertising or analytics SDKs; the app records no usage analytics. No account. If someone asks for score updates by email, we keep their email address and the postcode they follow, once they confirm by email.

6. AGE RATING
No age-restricted content: 4+.
