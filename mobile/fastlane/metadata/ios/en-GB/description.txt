Sky Score shows what the official figures say about a place: aircraft and road noise, air quality, flood risk and the planes overhead, beside prices, crime, schools, transport and healthcare. Search a postcode, a place or a council area. Every figure names its source and its year.

WHAT YOU GET

• An answer card for each postcode and council area: what stands out first, then the figures as tiles you can open.
• Aircraft and road noise from DEFRA's strategic noise maps (Round 4, mapped for 2021), modelled for the postcode and set against the WHO guideline. Where DEFRA does not map aircraft noise, an estimate from the published flight routes, labelled as one.
• Planes overhead: the planes counted over each postcode district, at airports whose counts pass a check against the CAA's monthly totals.
• Air quality: DEFRA's modelled figure for the postcode's own 1 km square, beside the nearest monitor and sensor readings.
• Flood risk from the Environment Agency's maps.
• Map layers for aircraft noise, road noise, flood risk, air quality, flight routes and the planes overhead.
• A score out of 10 for quiet skies, affordability, price growth and liveability, weighted for the profile you choose: Balanced, Family, Investor, First-Time, Quiet Life or Renter.
• Rankings of council areas, and a Saved list for the places you want to come back to.

COVERAGE

97 council areas in 13 UK city regions, plus New York's 5 boroughs and 50 Bay Area cities.

HOW IT WORKS

The method and every source are published at skyscore.co.uk/how-it-works. A modelled figure is called modelled; only a monitor's reading is called measured. This is information, not advice: not a survey, a valuation or a conveyancing search.

PRIVACY

No ads, and no tracking across apps or websites. If you ask for score updates by email, we keep your address and the postcode you follow; we email you once to confirm, and nothing more until you do. The privacy policy has the full detail.

SOURCES

Every figure names its source in the app. Among them: DEFRA, the Environment Agency, the Office for National Statistics, HM Land Registry, the Department for Education, the NHS, the Department for Transport, the UK AIP (NATS), the Civil Aviation Authority, adsb.lol flight tracks (ODbL) and Breathe London; in the US, the FAA (provided "as is", without warranty), FEMA and the US Census Bureau.
