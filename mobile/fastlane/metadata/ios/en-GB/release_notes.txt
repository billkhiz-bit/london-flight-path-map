This update brings the app in line with the Sky Score website.

• More places: 97 council areas in 13 UK city regions, plus New York's 5 boroughs and 50 Bay Area cities.
• An answer card for every postcode and council area: what stands out first, then the figures as tiles you can open.
• Planes overhead: the planes counted over each postcode district, at airports whose counts pass a check against the CAA's monthly totals.
• Air quality nearby: DEFRA's modelled figure for the postcode's own 1 km square, beside the nearest monitor and sensor readings.
• Every figure names its source and its year.
• Security and stability updates.
