Your aircraft noise report, free

One page for your own home: the official aircraft noise level at the postcode, the published flight routes that pass it and how high the aircraft are there. It is made in your browser from public data and is free to read on screen.

Before you rely on it

  • It covers postcodes in the city regions on the map. Outside them the report says so rather than guess.
  • The noise level is DEFRA's official map, which models 2021, when COVID cut air traffic; levels today are likely to be higher. Where DEFRA has no reading the report shows our estimate and says it is one.
  • It says where the routes are, not how many aircraft use them.
  • It is information, not a survey or a conveyancing search. Visit at different times of day before deciding.
