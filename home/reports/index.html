How a report is made

Official sources onlyDEFRA's noise and air maps, the aeronautical publications the routes are drawn from, and the Environment Agency's flood map. Each figure carries its source and date.
Checked before it leavesThe same checks that gate the website run on the data a report reads: prices against HM Land Registry, crime against the Office for National Statistics, flood against the Environment Agency's own service.
Honest about estimatesWhere the noise map has no reading for a street, the report says so and shows our estimate from the published routes as an estimate, never as a measurement.
What it does not sayHow often aircraft fly a route: the official files say where the routes are, not their traffic. And nothing about a property we have not measured.
