3. Accuracy and the limits of the data

Sky Score is derived from third-party public datasets, each listed with its source and licence in LICENSING.md. We do not control those datasets and we cannot guarantee them.

Published data lags reality, sometimes by years. Official datasets are released on their own schedules and we publish the vintage of each one in our methodology. Where a dataset is known to be anomalous or unusually old, we say so there. A score describes the data we hold, not the state of a place today.

Scores change. They change when a source dataset is refreshed and when our methodology is revised. The methodology is versioned like software and every change is recorded in our changelog. A score you saw previously may not be the score you see now, and neither is a promise about the future.
