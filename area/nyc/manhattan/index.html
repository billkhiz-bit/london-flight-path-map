Where these numbers come from

Borough metadata: curated from public New York City sources; Crime: NYPD CompStat-derived offence rates per 1,000, against New York City population denominators; Prices: curated New York borough median sale prices, in USD; Aviation noise context: ESTIMATED from the FAA routes of JFK, LaGuardia, Newark and Teterboro, fitted to the Port Authority of New York and New Jersey noise-monitor readings for January-July 2026; NOT a measurement at this address; Borough aircraft band: the same estimate averaged over residents by 2020 ZIP Code Tabulation Area, weighted by US Census Bureau ACS 2020-2024 population (public domain); Licence note: Open Government Licence v3.0 covers UK Crown copyright and does NOT apply to any data in this response; Flight routes: FAA Coded Instrument Flight Procedures, derived by Sky Score for the aircraft noise estimate; supplied "as is", without warranty, and not for navigation; Runway use: counted by Sky Score from aircraft positions via adsb.lol (ODbL 1.0), 2026-01-01 to 2026-07-30; the counts are offered under the ODbL

Methodology version 5.7. Full method: the methodology (and the API reference). Figures are for the whole borough; a single address can differ, and the map shows postcode-level detail.
