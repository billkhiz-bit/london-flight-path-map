Where these numbers come from

Prices: HM Land Registry UK House Price Index, May 2026 vintage, Open Government Licence v3.0; Aviation noise context: ESTIMATED from Bristol Airport (BRS) runway 09/27 geometry, with the distance ladder scaled by its measured DEFRA Round 4 55 dB Lden footprint; NOT a DEFRA sample at this address; Crime: ONS Crime in England and Wales, Police Force Area data tables, year ending March 2026, Open Government Licence v3.0; Liveability (4 of 4 inputs): DfE Key Stage 4 Progress 8, 2022/23 Revised; ONS Crime in England and Wales, Table C4; NaPTAN rail, metro and tram access within 800 m; NHS Organisation Data Service GP practices within 500 m. Any absent input has its weight redistributed, not defaulted

Methodology version 3.8. Full method: API reference. Figures are for the whole borough; a single address can differ, and the map shows postcode-level detail.
