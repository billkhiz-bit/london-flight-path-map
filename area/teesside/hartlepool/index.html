Where these numbers come from

Prices: HM Land Registry UK House Price Index, May 2026 vintage, Open Government Licence v3.0; Aviation noise context: ESTIMATED from Teesside International Airport (MME) runway 05/23 geometry, with the distance ladder floored at the smallest published DEFRA Round 4 footprint because this airport is below the threshold DEFRA maps at all; NOT a DEFRA sample at this address; Crime: ONS Crime in England and Wales, Police Force Area data tables, year ending March 2026, Open Government Licence v3.0. Cleveland for four authorities and Durham for Darlington, this city region spanning two forces; Liveability (4 of 4 inputs): DfE Key Stage 4 Progress 8, 2022/23 Revised; ONS Crime in England and Wales, Table C4; NaPTAN rail, metro and tram access within 800 m; NHS Organisation Data Service GP practices within 500 m. Any absent input has its weight redistributed, not defaulted

Methodology version 3.8. Full method: API reference. Figures are for the whole borough; a single address can differ, and the map shows postcode-level detail.
