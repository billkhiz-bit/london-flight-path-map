What this is: DEFRA Strategic Noise Map (Round 4, published 2022), the long-term average aircraft noise around LHR, LCY and LGW - modelled from a year of actual flight tracks, not a live feed. That year is 2021, when COVID restrictions held air traffic far below normal, and DEFRA describes the Round 4 result as a highly anomalous situation. These contours therefore understate a typical year, most at the airports worst affected. Round 4 is still the latest official round; DEFRA expects Round 5 around 2027. Used by councils for planning decisions. The overlay shows DEFRA's modelled contours; a postcode search gives a 0-10 quiet score for that location, not a decibel reading.
