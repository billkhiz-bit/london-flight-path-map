## Summary

- Critical: 1 (C-1)
- Important: 8 (I-8 is a decision)
- Minor: 20
- Nothing in this audit changes a published score. The Critical and four of the Importants are a page failing to show or say the right thing; `/preview/` accounts for five of the eight Importants, which is what a trial is for.

**Pattern worth keeping:** C-1 is the 7 Sep audit's C2 again - a rule keyed on a marker whose meaning changed underneath it. `.empty-state` meant "nothing has been searched yet" when the hide rule was written, and came to mean "any panel without a result" as outcome messages were added. And its gate is the "measure the DOM, not the flag" lesson in a new form: `innerText` of an element that is not rendered returns the text anyway.
