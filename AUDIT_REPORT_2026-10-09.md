## Checked and found sound

- **9 Oct work:** the routes row by keyboard at five viewports (Tab to an area, Enter, focus on its `h2`, one Tab to a 26 px summary with a 3.8:1 ring, a real `<ul>`, Escape back to the area, no trap); its geometry (even-odd inside test, zero-length and collinear edges handled). The QR is byte-identical to npm's qrcode-generator 2.0.4 and decodes on the live site at 1x and 1.5x.
- **Security:** no XSS, open redirect or secret in the window. The street report validates postcode, `made` and `ref` by pattern before use and escapes every API string; its srcdoc frame inherits the CSP (an injected inline script and an `onerror` were both blocked, live). `?city` uses `Object.hasOwn` on the front page; the CSV parser cannot pollute a prototype; `#oskey` is pattern-checked and stripped. CSPs on `/`, `/reports/`, `/reports/street/`, 404 and the forwards have no `'unsafe-inline'` script; 404 and the forwards are `default-src 'none'`. The CloudFront function's 301 targets are constants (`/privacy.html?x=//evil.com` -> `/privacy`). Every page checked carries HSTS, nosniff, XFO DENY, Referrer-Policy and Permissions-Policy; Lambda 400/404 bodies carry HSTS + nosniff. GoatCounter's integrity hash matches the served file. `npm audit`: 0 at the root, 0 in `mobile/`. No keys, account IDs or members of the public in the diff.
- **Correctness:** the Lambda's AIP provenance lines resolve for all 14 cities; `shownDistances` airport codes match for every city; `build_nyc_front.py --check` passes; Makefile keys right; `sw.js`'s best-effort `/map/` precache and offline fallback right; no relative asset URL left in the map at `/map/`. `tests/front-page.mjs` 196/196.
- **Accessibility:** axe (WCAG 2.2 AA + best-practice) clean on the front page landing, with a card and its routes open, and with an answer open, at five viewports, every contrast "incomplete" measured by hand; clean on `/reports/`, `/pricing`, privacy, terms, changes, talks, status, `/api/`, `/open-data/` bar the findings above. No page scrolls sideways at any viewport; every target is >= 24 px or excepted; one `main`, one banner, one `h1` on each page; results announced in a live region.
