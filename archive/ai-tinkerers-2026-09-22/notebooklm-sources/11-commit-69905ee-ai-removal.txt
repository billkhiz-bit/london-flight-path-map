Remove all AI features from frontend (option 3)

Strips the AI chat panel, AI insight auto-summary, multi-agent routing,
property-photo image analysis, EPC/survey doc analysis, and AI-generated
property report. Five Bedrock-spending features gone from the consumer site.

Reasoning: methodology defensibility is the B2B story. Tacking AI summaries
on top of deterministic scoring introduces variance that B2B audit teams
will challenge first, and the "not fully accurate" complaint is structural
not tunable. AWS-credit savings are the secondary win (~$80-115/month at
modest traffic).

The 5 AI Lambdas (chat, multi_agent, analyze_image, analyze_document, report)
and their template.yaml entries stay intact — Lambda has zero idle cost on
on-demand pricing, and re-introducing any feature later is just unhiding
the UI block. Deliberately kept dormant rather than deleted so a constrained
"explain in plain English" button (user-triggered, not auto-firing) remains
a one-commit re-enable if consumer feedback warrants it.

Removals:
- HTML: chat-fab, chat-panel, report-modal, AI insight section in sidebar,
  EPC/survey upload UI, "Generate AI Report" button.
- CSS: all .chat-*, .report-modal/.report-content/.report-close/.report-print,
  .upload-area, .chat-upload-btn, #ai-insight-* (~110 lines).
- JS: chatHistory, currentViewingContext, currentReportData, MULTI_AGENT_KEYWORDS,
  shouldUseMultiAgent, sendChatMessage, fetchInsight, formatChatReply,
  analyzePropertyImage, analyzeDocument, generateReport, formatReportHtml,
  closeReport, printReport, openPrintableWindow, handleDocUpload, setChatWelcome,
  plus init handlers (~280 lines).
- All "Powered by Amazon Nova" / "Nova Pro" branding strings (now moot).

Net: -25 KB on the served HTML, JS parses clean, 12 lint warnings dropped
along with removed code.
