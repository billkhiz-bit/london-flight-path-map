Sky Score - Demo Video Script (2-3 minutes)
=============================================

OPENING (10 seconds)
Show the app loaded on London view.

"Sky Score is a free AI-powered tool that helps property buyers assess aircraft noise before they commit. It covers London and New York with over 290 individually scored neighbourhoods. Whether you're a first-time buyer, a family, a renter, an investor, or just looking for a quiet life - Sky Score scores every area to match your priorities."

SEARCH A POSTCODE (20 seconds)
Type TW3 (Hounslow) and hit search. Show the sidebar data loading - noise assessment, buyer score, crime, schools, transport.

"Search any postcode and get an instant analysis - noise level, distance to airports, flight paths, crime, schools, transport, and an AI-generated insight."

MAP LAYERS (20 seconds)
Toggle on Flight Paths, then Aircraft Noise overlay. Toggle Flood Risk - show the blue borough coloring. Toggle Air Quality - show the red/amber/green borough coloring. Toggle Road Noise and zoom into a borough to see the detailed DEFRA contours appear.

"Toggle data layers directly on the map. Flood risk and air quality show borough-level ratings at a glance. Zoom in and road noise contours from DEFRA appear street by street. All data comes from official government sources."

RANKINGS + PERSONAS (20 seconds)
Click the Rankings tab. Show the neighbourhood list. Switch persona from Balanced to Family - show scores recalculate.

"Every neighbourhood is individually scored using actual distance to airports, local property prices, crime data, and transport links. Switch between five buyer personas - Balanced, Family, Investor, First-Time, and Quiet Life - and all 290 scores recalculate instantly."

MULTI-AGENT CHAT (30 seconds)
Open the chat. Type: "Compare Hounslow vs Richmond for a family with a 600K budget"

Wait for the multi-agent typing indicator to appear - pause on it briefly. Then show the response with agent badges.

"Complex questions activate our multi-agent system. An orchestrator decomposes the query, three specialist agents - Noise Analyst, Property Researcher, and Neighbourhood Scorer - analyse in parallel, then Nova Pro synthesises a unified recommendation. Simpler questions go straight to Nova 2 Lite for a fast response."

CITY SWITCH (10 seconds)
Click New York. Show the map switch. Search Astoria or 11102.

"Switch to New York instantly. Same scoring engine, same AI, different data sources - BTS noise data, FEMA flood zones, Zillow and StreetEasy listings."

PHOTO ANALYSIS (20 seconds)
Open chat, click the upload button, upload a property photo. Show the Nova Pro analysis appearing.

"Upload a property listing photo and Nova Pro analyses the condition, glazing type - critical for noise insulation - and flags any visible issues."

DOCUMENT ANALYSIS (15 seconds)
Navigate to document analysis or upload a PDF in chat. Use the sample EPC PDF. Show the extraction.

"Upload an EPC certificate or building survey and Nova Pro extracts the key data - energy rating, insulation, estimated costs, and improvement recommendations."

GENERATE REPORT (15 seconds)
Search a location, click Generate Report. Show the report modal opening with the 7 sections.

"One click generates a full 7-section Property Intelligence Report covering noise, market analysis, amenities, risks, investment outlook, and a verdict."

CLOSING (10 seconds)
Zoom out to show the full map.

"Sky Score. Free, no sign-up, live now. Built with Amazon Nova on AWS."

Show the URL: d1oe4ftwutjpf.cloudfront.net

RECORDING TIPS
- Keep each section tight - if over 3 minutes, cut the document analysis section
- Pause briefly on multi-agent badges and typing indicator - judges look for this
- Record at 1080p or higher
- Use OBS Studio or Windows Game Bar (Win+G) to screen record
- Add voiceover live or record separately and overlay
